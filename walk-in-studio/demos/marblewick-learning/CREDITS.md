# Image credits - Marblewick Learning Hub

Demo site for a made-up tuition centre. Every photo below is free-licence stock (Pexels), resized and compressed to
WebP by `tools/make_images.py`. They are NOT photos of the centre, its tutors or its students and are labelled "sample" on
the site (GROUND_RULES Rule 10). No photo shows a child's face (no minors without the owner's written OK).

Fonts: self-hosted open-licence fonts, see "Fonts" below.

## Fonts
- `assets/fonts/bricolage-grotesque-700.woff2`: Bricolage Grotesque 700 (OFL-1.1, headings), Latin subset, self-hosted from Fontsource (fontsource.org)
- `assets/fonts/nunito-*.woff2`: Nunito 400/700/800 (OFL-1.1, body and option C), Latin subset, self-hosted from Fontsource (fontsource.org)
- `assets/fonts/source-serif-4-600.woff2`: Source Serif 4 600 (OFL-1.1, option B headings), Latin subset, self-hosted from Fontsource (fontsource.org)
- `assets/fonts/ibm-plex-mono-400.woff2`: IBM Plex Mono 400 (OFL-1.1, option D times), Latin subset, self-hosted from Fontsource (fontsource.org)
