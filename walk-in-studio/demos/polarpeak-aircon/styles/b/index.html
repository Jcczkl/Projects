How a visit works

Four steps, every time.

The same order on every job, whether it is one unit or five.

  1. Message us

    WhatsApp your area and how many units. A photo of the unit helps.

  2. We confirm

    The price per unit and a slot. Same-day slots when free.

  3. We service

    Two technicians, floor covers down, vacuum when we finish.

  4. You check

    Feel the cold air, then pay by e-wallet or cash.
