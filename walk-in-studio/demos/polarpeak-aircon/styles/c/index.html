Booking

Booking is just a chat.

Tell us your area and how many units. A photo of the unit helps. The price comes in our first reply, and you only pay after you have felt the cold air.

Start a chat

Example chat

  1. Hi, I’m in Yishun. 3 aircon units need servicing.
  2. Hi! General servicing is $30 a unit, so $90 for 3. No call-out fee. When is good for you?
  3. Saturday morning?
  4. Saturday is fine. Two of us will come with floor covers and a vacuum. You test the cold air, then pay by e-wallet or cash.
