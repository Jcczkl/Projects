Before your visit

How do I book?

WhatsApp us your preferred day and time and the look you’d like. We reply with the free slots.

How long does a gel set last?

Usually two to three weeks, depending on your nails and what you use your hands for.

Can I bring a design photo?

Please do. We’ll tell you honestly what’s possible and the price before we start.

What if I need to reschedule?

Just let us know a day ahead so we can offer the slot to someone else.
