The dough

Two days to rise. Ninety seconds to bake.

Flour, water, salt and our own sourdough starter. Nothing else. Each dough ball rests for 48 hours, gets stretched by hand when you order, and goes into a wood fire at around 450°C. It comes out blistered outside, soft inside and a little smoky.

  • 48-hour sourdough
  • Stretched by hand
  • Wood fire, about 450°C

Good to know

Do I need to book?

Not for small groups on weeknights. Friday and weekend evenings fill up, so a WhatsApp message the day before is safer.

Do you have vegetarian pizzas?

Yes: the Margherita, Marinara and Funghi, plus the burrata starter and the tiramisu. They are marked V on the menu.

Do you deliver?

No. Pizza from a wood fire goes soft in a delivery box, so we do takeaway only: order on WhatsApp and collect.
