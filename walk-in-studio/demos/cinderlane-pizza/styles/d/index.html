Cinderlane Pizza · Upper Thomson

Every pizza here starts two days before you sit down.

Follow one ball of sourdough from the mixing bowl to the wood fire to your table. Pizzas from $16; dinner Tuesday to Sunday, lunch at weekends.

01 · Mix

Flour, water, salt and our own starter.

Nothing else goes into the dough. The starter is our own sourdough culture: it does all the rising, slowly.

02 · Rest

Then we wait two days.

Each dough ball rests for 48 hours. The slow rise is what gives the crust its light, blistered bite.

03 · Stretch

Stretched by hand, when you order.

Your turn

Book your table.

WhatsApp us the day, time and how many. Walk-ins are welcome; for 8 or more, message us a day ahead.

Book a table Order takeaway

Takeaway: order on WhatsApp, collect in about 20 minutes. We don’t deliver.

Do I need to book?

Not for small groups on weeknights. Friday and weekend evenings fill up, so a WhatsApp message the day before is safer.

Do you have vegetarian pizzas?

Yes: the Margherita, Marinara and Funghi, plus the burrata starter and the tiramisu. They are marked V on the menu.

Do you deliver?

No. Pizza from a wood fire goes soft in a delivery box, so we do takeaway only: order on WhatsApp and collect.
