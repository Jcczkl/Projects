Do I need to book?

Not for small groups on weeknights. Friday and weekend evenings fill up, so a WhatsApp message the day before is safer.

Do you have vegetarian pizzas?

Yes: the Margherita, Marinara and Funghi, plus the burrata starter and the tiramisu. They are marked V on the menu.

Do you deliver?

No. Pizza from a wood fire goes soft in a delivery box, so we do takeaway only: order on WhatsApp and collect.
