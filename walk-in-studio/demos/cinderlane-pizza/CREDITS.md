# Image credits - cinderlane-pizza

Demo site for a made-up shop (Cinderlane Pizza). Every image below is free-licence stock (Pexels), resized and
compressed to WebP by the restaurant kit (`template/kits/restaurant/`). They are NOT photos of the shop and are
labelled "Sample photo" on every page and in every alt text (GROUND_RULES Rule 10). Style options B-D
(`styles/<x>/`) use the same files; they add no images.

Built 2026-10-10 from the restaurant kit with `tools/new_site.py demos cinderlane-pizza --trade restaurant`.
