Key filing dates

When are your IRAS deadlines?

Pick your financial year end. We’ll list the main corporate tax and GST dates that follow from it.

  1. With JavaScript off: ECI is due 3 months after your financial year end; Form C-S / C is due 30 Nov of the following year; quarterly GST returns are due one month after each quarter ends.

A general guide, not advice. Confirm your own dates with us or IRAS. GST quarters are set by IRAS (see your GST registration letter).

No ECI is needed if revenue is $5 million or below and ECI is nil. For YA 2023–2027, IRAS allows Form C-S filed through accounting software until 15 Dec.

Sources: IRAS pages on ECI filing, Form C-S / C filing and GST due dates.
