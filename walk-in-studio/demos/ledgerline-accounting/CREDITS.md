# Image and video credits - ledgerline-accounting

Demo site for a made-up shop. Every image below is free-licence stock, downloaded
2026-10-06, resized and compressed (WebP). They are NOT photos of the shop.

No video. Icons and illustrations are inline SVG drawn for this site.
`assets/img/og.jpg` is a text card rendered from HTML for this demo.

Icons: inline SVG drawn for the template; WhatsApp glyph from Simple Icons (CC0).
Fonts: see "Fonts" below (self-hosted, open licences). Option A sets headings and body in Cabin; options B-D use their own display face with a system body font.
