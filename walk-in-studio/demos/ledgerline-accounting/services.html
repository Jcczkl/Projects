Services

Everything between your receipts and IRAS.

What’s included, written plainly, so you know exactly what you are paying for.

Bookkeeping

  • Sales, purchases and expenses posted every month
  • Bank and credit card reconciliations
  • Receivables and payables kept current
  • Management report: profit and loss, balance sheet, cash position

GST

  • Advice on whether and when you must register
  • Quarterly GST F5 returns prepared and filed
  • Input tax claims checked against your invoices
  • Reminders before every due date

Corporate income tax

  • Estimated Chargeable Income (ECI), or confirming you qualify for the waiver
  • Tax computation and Form C-S, C-S (Lite) or C
  • Replies to routine IRAS queries on returns we filed

Payroll

  • Monthly payslips and payroll summary
  • CPF contribution calculations
  • Year-end IR8A forms for employees

Year-end accounts

  • Unaudited financial statements for companies that don’t need an audit
  • Working with your auditor if your company does need one

What we don’t do

  • Company secretarial services — we work alongside your company secretary
  • Audits
  • Personal investment or financial advice
