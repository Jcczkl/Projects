# Credits - Bobbinmere Tailoring

A made-up shop, built to show the Signature style. Every picture is free-licence stock (or the template's own
files), not the shop's; the page says so in its banner.

## Images and footage
- `assets/img/sig-ink.png`: the ink-dissolve mask of the Signature kit, rendered by `tools/make_ink_mask.py` (SVG noise; no source image).
- `assets/img/og.jpg`: the template's blank share image (replace it with a screenshot of this page's own first screen before launch).
- `assets/fonts/*.woff2`: Poppins by the Poppins Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`assets/fonts/OFL.txt`).
- `assets/img/sig/draw-jacket.svg`: a drawing made for Walk-In Studio (tools/store_art.py), not a photo; nothing to credit.
- `assets/img/sig/draw-trousers.svg`: a drawing made for Walk-In Studio (tools/store_art.py), not a photo; nothing to credit.
- `assets/img/sig/draw-shirt.svg`: a drawing made for Walk-In Studio (tools/store_art.py), not a photo; nothing to credit.
