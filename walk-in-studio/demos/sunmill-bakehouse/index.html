Before you order

How early should I order a birthday cake?

Three days ahead for our standard cakes. For custom designs or tiered cakes, a week is safer, especially near school holidays.

Do you deliver?

Cakes are for collection from the shop. If you need delivery, we can help you book a courier and you pay them directly.

Can you make it less sweet?

Most of our cream and frosting can be made lighter. Just mention it when you order.
