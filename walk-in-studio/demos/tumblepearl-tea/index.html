Good to know

Do you deliver?

No. We shake every cup to order, so it’s takeaway only: order on WhatsApp and pick it up.

Can I change the sugar and ice?

Yes, on every drink: sugar from 0% to 100%, and no ice, less ice, normal ice or warm.

Are the pearls made here?

We cook them here in small batches through the day, so they’re soft when you get them.
