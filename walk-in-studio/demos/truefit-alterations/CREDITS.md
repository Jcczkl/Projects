# Image credits - truefit-alterations

Demo site for a made-up shop. Every image below is free-licence stock (Pexels), resized
and compressed to WebP with `tools/make_images.py` (quality 80). They are NOT photos of the
business and are labelled "sample photo" on the site (GROUND_RULES Rule 10).

Updated 2026-10-07: walk-in removed; craft hero and standout pieces added (round 3: one muted, warm colour grade across all photos, ffmpeg eq/colorbalance). The before/after is a drawing, not a photo.
