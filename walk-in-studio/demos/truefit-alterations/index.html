Process

Three steps, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Bring it in

    Wear the shoes you’ll wear with it. We pin it on you and tell you the price before we start.

  2. 02

    We alter it

    Most jobs are ready in 3 working days. Zips and buttons often the same day.

  3. 03

    Try it on

    Try it on when you collect. If it isn’t right, we fix it at no charge.
