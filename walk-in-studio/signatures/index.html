Signature

All signatures, trade by trade.

Two kinds of Signature homepage: the product as the star, or a walk through the shop. Every example below is a made-up shop.

Shown here with drawings and sample photos to show the style.

Signature · Product The product is the star: a short story for the trade, then the products.

Signature · Place The place is the star: push the door and walk through the shop, stop by stop.
