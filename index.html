I ran five more searches — over 650 rule sets, fund-switching schemes and a self-learning model.

None passed. The closest failed a fairness check: random decisions did just as well.

Simply holding a market fund beat everything I built.

A trading YouTuber's own published figures, which argue against his video's framing. Source ↗

  • Pass criteria written down and saved before any search ran — never lowered after.
  • Every rule had to beat two fairness checks: random trades, and its own decisions scrambled.

Decide what counts as success after seeing the results, and almost anything looks like a win.

Simple patterns in public price data are the most crowded corner of the market. Firms with more of these found them first:

  • Speed — servers inside the exchange.
  • Breadth — thousands of instruments, far more data.
  • Scale — enormous capital on thin, known edges.

A clean negative from a careful process is the process working.
